The first public version of TeleNotch.

### Teleprompter in the notch
- Your script scrolls in a black panel that grows out of the notch, right under the camera.
- Speed in words per minute, jump back, mirror mode, adjustable size.

### Meeting copilot
- Listens to your mic (you) and your Mac's audio (everyone else) and transcribes both on your Mac.
- **Answer for me (⌃⌥↩):** a short answer in your voice that takes a clear side, ready to read aloud.
- **Catch me up (⌃⌥C):** three lines on what's being discussed and what you were asked.
- Meeting and presentation modes, plus "About me" so answers sound like you.

### Easy to use
- A welcome guide for your API key, permissions and a demo answer.
- Buttons and shortcut hints right in the notch; ⌃⌥/ shows every shortcut.
- Updates install themselves.
