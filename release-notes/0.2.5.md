Listening you can rely on in a real meeting, and only your voice on your side.

- **Only hears your voice.** Your mic now keeps just the voice closest to it. A colleague talking nearby, office chatter and background noise no longer turn into made-up words under your name. You can turn this off in Settings → Copilot ("Only hear my voice").
- **No more double lines.** When someone in the same room is also on the call, their words show up once, from the call, instead of a second, half-misheard time from your mic.
- **Knows who's who.** Add the people in your meeting in Settings ("Who's in this meeting"). Claude uses the names, who answers whom and what each person has argued to tell the "Others" apart. Notes keep a line per person, and Catch me up says who's arguing what.
- **Fixed:** with Bluetooth headphones, listening could get stuck on "Starting…" or stop with "Couldn't read the audio format". TeleNotch now records your Mac's microphone directly.
- **Fixed:** crashes when the pointer touched the notch after you started listening.
- **Fixed:** the shortcuts could stop working until you quit and reopened TeleNotch.
