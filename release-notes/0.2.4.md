A smoother notch, and listening that keeps working with Bluetooth headphones.

- **The notch opens like Apple's.** It grows out of the camera notch and tucks back in when you hide it, with curved corners where it meets the top of the screen.
- **Pause by pointing.** Move your pointer onto the notch and the script stops; move it away and it carries on.
- **Bluetooth headphones just work.** TeleNotch now uses your Mac's own microphone when your headphones are the default mic, so they don't drop into low-quality call mode and transcription keeps going.
- **Keeps listening when devices change.** Connecting headphones or switching speakers restarts TeleNotch's audio by itself. If no audio comes in, the notch tells you.
- **Fixed:** a crash when the pointer moved over the notch.
- **Fixed:** on a fresh install the script started at 0 words per minute and didn't scroll.
