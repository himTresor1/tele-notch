What TeleNotch does

TeleNotch has two parts that share the same black panel at the top of your screen.

The teleprompter

Paste a script and it scrolls in the notch at the speed you set, in words per minute. Because the text sits right under the camera, you keep looking at the people you're talking to while you read. The panel asks macOS to leave it out of screen shares and recordings.

The meeting copilot

Turn on Listen and TeleNotch transcribes your microphone (Me) and everything your Mac plays (Others: Zoom, Meet, Teams, a video, anything). Transcription happens on your Mac. While you listen, Claude keeps short running notes of the meeting.

When you get asked something and lose the thread, press ⌃⌥↩. Claude writes a two-to-four-sentence answer, in the first person and in your voice, that takes a clear side. It appears in the notch so you can read it out straight away.

Who it's for

  • Presenters who want their notes under the camera, not on another screen.
  • People in long meetings who drift off for a minute and get asked for their view.
  • Debates and discussions where you want a clear position fast.

TeleNotch is not meant for job interviews or exams. Use it where getting help is fair, and tell people if you're transcribing them: in some places everyone on a call has to agree to it.

What you need

  • A Mac running macOS 26 or later. It's made for MacBooks with a notch, where the panel grows out of the notch.
  • Your own Anthropic API key for the copilot. You pay Anthropic directly for what you use; TeleNotch itself is free. The teleprompter works without a key.
  • Headphones for meetings. With speakers, your mic picks up the other people too.

Privacy

Audio never leaves your Mac, and transcripts stay on it. Recent transcript text is sent to Claude only when you press Answer or Catch up, and every 45 seconds for the running notes while you're listening. Your API key is stored in your Mac's Keychain.
