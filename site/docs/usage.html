Guides

How to Use

Everything happens in the black panel at the top of your screen. Click its buttons, or keep your hands on the keyboard: every shortcut is ⌃ Control + ⌥ Option + one key.

The notch panel

Press ⌃⌥Space to show or hide the panel. You can also click the ⌃ button at its top right, or use the TeleNotch icon in the menu bar.

  • Left of the camera: the Listen button, play / pause, and the scroll speed.
  • Right of the camera: the Answer button, the ⋯ menu with everything else, and the hide button.
  • Along the bottom: the shortcuts that make sense right now. You never have to memorise them.

Messages, such as a missing API key or a permission that's off, appear at the bottom of the panel with a button to fix them.

Teleprompter

Add your script

Press ⌃⌥E (or ⋯ → Edit Script…) and paste or type your script. It saves as you type. Click Start Scrolling when you're ready.

Control the scrolling

ShortcutWhat it does
⌃⌥PPlay or pause.
⌃⌥↑ ⌃⌥↓Faster or slower, 10 words per minute at a time. The speed shows in the panel; 140 wpm is a relaxed speaking pace.
⌃⌥←Jump back a few lines.

To start over from the top, choose Restart Script from the menu bar icon.

Make it fit

In Settings (⌃⌥,) you can change the text size and the panel's width and height, mirror the text for a beam-splitter teleprompter, and turn the teal glow behind the text on or off.

Meeting copilot

Start listening

Press ⌃⌥L or click Listen. TeleNotch transcribes your mic as Me and your Mac's audio as Others, and keeps running notes of the meeting with Claude. Press it again to stop.

Use headphones. With speakers, your mic also picks up the other people. TeleNotch filters most of the echo, but headphones work best.

Answer for me

When someone asks you something, press ⌃⌥↩ or click Answer. Within about a second, an answer starts appearing in the notch, marked Say this. It's written in the first person, as you, and takes a clear side, so you can read it out as it is.

Press ⌃⌥↩ again, or click ↩ Script, to go back to your script.

Catch me up

Drifted off? Press ⌃⌥C for three short lines: what's being discussed, where it's heading, and whether anyone asked you something.

Transcript and new meetings

⋯ → Live Transcript… shows everything that's been said. ⋯ → New Meeting clears the transcript and notes, so the next answer doesn't mix up two meetings.

Better answers

Answers get better when Claude knows who you are and what the meeting is about. Open Settings (⌃⌥,) and fill in:

  • About me: your name, your role, your views, and how you talk.
  • This meeting: the agenda, the goals, and who's there.

Meeting or presentation mode

Pick a mode in Settings:

  • Meeting: answers draw on the conversation and your notes.
  • Presentation: answers respond to the question you just heard. Your script is used only as background, so you're not pushed back to your slides.

You can also choose the Claude model. Faster models put the answer on screen sooner; Opus gives the most thoughtful answers.

Screen sharing and privacy

The panel asks macOS to keep it out of screen shares and recordings, so the people you share your screen with don't see your script or answers. Check it once with your own meeting app before you rely on it.

Audio stays on your Mac. Transcript text goes to Claude only when you press Answer or Catch up, and for the running notes while you're listening. Tell people when you're transcribing them: in some places, everyone on a call has to agree to it.
