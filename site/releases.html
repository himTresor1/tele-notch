Install in four steps

  1. Download and open TeleNotch.dmg A window opens with TeleNotch and your Applications folder.
  2. Drag TeleNotch onto Applications Then eject the disk image (the ⏏ next to “TeleNotch” in Finder's sidebar).
  3. Open TeleNotch from Applications macOS will say “TeleNotch” Not Opened. That's expected for apps that aren't notarized by Apple yet. Click Done, not Move to Trash.
  4. Allow it once in System Settings Open System Settings → Privacy & Security, scroll down to Security, and next to “TeleNotch” was blocked click Open Anyway. Enter your Mac password, then click Open.

You only do this once. A short guide then sets up your Anthropic API key and permissions, and TeleNotch updates itself from then on.

Comfortable with Terminal?

After step 2, this one command clears the download flag so TeleNotch opens without the warning. Only do this for apps you trust.

xattr -dr com.apple.quarantine /Applications/TeleNotch.app
